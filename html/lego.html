LEGO Sumo

What is LEGO Sumo?

Build a Robot. Push to Win.

Working with a partner, elementary schoolers build their own LEGO Sumo robot from the kit, then compete head-to-head to push opponents out of the ring — no experience required, just curiosity.

CryptoHawks FRC mentors guide every step, from first build to final match. Proceeds directly support Team 8726's competitive season.
