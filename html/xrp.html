What is Mountain Mayhem?

Build a Robot. Compete for the Summit.

Working with a partner, students in grades 6–8 build their own XRP robot from the kit, then compete head-to-head in the official Mountain Mayhem game — no experience required, just curiosity.

CryptoHawks FRC mentors guide every step, from first assembly to final match. Proceeds directly support Team 8726's competitive season.
