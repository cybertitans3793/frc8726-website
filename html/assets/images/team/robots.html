Offseason

BunnyBots

🐰

Blair BunnyBots is an offseason event hosted by FRC Team 449 at Montgomery Blair High School each fall. Teams participate in a separate build season with a brand-new game designed by mentors and alumni — giving rookie members their first chance to build a real FRC-style robot and veterans a chance to mentor and lead. Adults don't touch the robot. Students do everything.
